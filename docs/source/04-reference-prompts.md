## Reference 1 — UGC product film (10 s, 9:16, 8 timed shots)

Create a 10-second vertical 9:16 UGC-style product film for the product shown in the attached reference image.

Before generating, silently identify the exact product category, intended user, visual style, colours, materials, and how the product would naturally be worn, handled or used. Use this analysis only to choose the appropriate talent, outfit, environment and actions.

The attached product is the hero product and its exact design must be preserved with absolute fidelity in every frame.

Every visible detail must remain exactly as shown in the reference image: identical silhouette, proportions, colour placement, materials, textures, stitching, seams, panels, hardware, fasteners, straps, laces, buttons, zippers, logos, labels, symbols, graphics, typography, surface finish, edges, openings, soles, tread, caps, lids, handles, screens, lenses, ports or any other visible component.

Do not redesign, simplify, improve, beautify, recolour or reinterpret the product.

Do not add any logo, brand name, label, tag, sticker, text, graphic, seam, stitch, strap, accessory, hardware element or product detail that is not present in the reference.

Do not remove any existing logo, branding, label, tag, text, stitching, panel, component or detail that is visible in the reference.

REFERENCE-ONLY BRANDING AND LABEL RULE — ABSOLUTE

The uploaded reference image is the only source of truth for all labels, logos, text and branding.

If the product in the reference image has NO label, generate the product with NO label.

If the product has exactly ONE label, preserve only that exact label. Do not add a second label, tag, sticker or printed element.

If the product has multiple labels, preserve only the labels that are genuinely visible in the reference.

If the product has NO logo, do not add any logo.

If the product has a logo, preserve only that same logo in the same location, approximate size, colour, orientation and visual style.

If the product has NO visible text, the generated product must contain NO text.

If the product has visible text, preserve only the actual visible text from the reference. Do not invent extra words, letters, numbers, slogans, product names or specifications.

If an area of the product is blank in the reference, it must remain blank.

Do not fill empty areas with branding.

Do not generate generic clothing labels, care labels, neck tags, shoe branding, bottle stickers, packaging labels, product badges or manufacturer marks unless they are genuinely visible in the uploaded reference.

Do not assume that a product “should” normally have a label, logo or text.

Do not add standard industry details based on the product category.

REFERENCE IMAGE OVERRIDES REAL-WORLD EXPECTATIONS.

What is visible in the reference stays.

What is absent from the reference stays absent.

Never invent branding to make the product look more realistic.

Never remove branding to make the product look cleaner.

Never replace unclear branding with a guessed or reconstructed brand.

When a logo, label or text is partially visible, preserve only what can reasonably be derived from the reference without inventing additional information.

If exact text cannot be reliably reproduced, preserve its visible graphic presence and placement without adding new readable wording.

The goal is not to create a “similar commercial product.”

The goal is to preserve the exact uploaded product.

If only one member of a natural pair is visible, such as one shoe, glove or earring, generate the correct matching counterpart only when necessary. The second item must match the original exactly and must not become a different variation.

The exact same product design must remain consistent across all eight shots. No detail may appear, disappear, move, change size or change colour between shots.

PRODUCT FIDELITY ALWAYS TAKES PRIORITY OVER CREATIVE STYLING.
TALENT AND OUTFIT

Do not automatically use the same woman, man, outfit or styling for every product.

Choose the talent and clothing based on the uploaded product itself.

The person should look like a believable user or customer for that specific product.

The product determines:

gender presentation when relevant
age range
wardrobe type
clothing silhouette
colours
footwear
accessories
grooming
overall style

The outfit must visually complement the hero product without competing with it.

For example, a premium men's leather shoe should not automatically be shown with grey joggers and a cream sweatshirt. It may require tailored trousers or refined smart-casual clothing.

A technical running shoe may require performance clothing.

A streetwear sneaker may require relaxed contemporary streetwear.

A women's fashion item may require styling appropriate to its design and customer.

A luxury handbag may require more polished fashion styling.

A watch, jewellery item, perfume, cosmetic, technology product or household object should each use talent and wardrobe that naturally fit the specific product.

Do not repeatedly default to a young woman wearing beige or grey clothing.

Each uploaded product must be treated as a new styling brief.

The clothing should support the product's colour palette, material, category, season, formality and intended use.

Do not recolour or modify the hero product to make it match the outfit.

Change the styling around the product, never the product around the styling.

TALENT VISIBILITY

Show only as much of the person as necessary.

For footwear, bags, watches, jewellery or other products where the face is unnecessary, the talent may be shown from the shoulders, chest, waist or legs down.

For beauty, eyewear, hair, cosmetics or products where facial context is important, the face may be visible when appropriate.

The product must always remain the dominant visual subject.

Movements should feel natural, relaxed and believable, like a real person using the product, not a model performing exaggerated poses.

WORLD AND SET

Choose a bright, clean, premium environment appropriate to the uploaded product.

Do not automatically reuse the same apartment for every product.

Depending on the product, the location may be a modern apartment, dressing area, bathroom, vanity, kitchen, living room, workspace, boutique-style interior, fitness space or another believable product-appropriate environment.

The environment should visually complement the product while keeping it clearly separated from the background.

Avoid unnecessary objects, unrelated branding and readable background text.

Use restrained colours so the hero product remains visually important.

CAMERA AND LENS

Handheld but stabilised, as if shot by an experienced UGC creator on a modern flagship phone or compact professional camera.

Use subtle natural micro-drift instead of perfectly locked tripod framing.

Use shallow depth of field on close shots when appropriate.

Use real macro visual behaviour for detail shots so materials and construction remain visible.

Show authentic textures such as leather grain, suede nap, fabric weave, stitching, rubber texture, metal finish, glass, plastic or other relevant materials.

Use natural handheld focus pulls.

No unnecessary zooms, whip pans, drone moves, extreme orbiting, speed ramps or artificial camera effects.

LIGHT AND GRADE

Use clean, premium, naturalistic advertising lighting.

Default to bright directional daylight with realistic bounce and soft dimensional shadows.

Adapt lighting to the material when necessary.

Textured products may use slight side light to reveal surface detail.

Metal or glass may use controlled highlights.

Dark products must remain separated from the background.

Light products must not disappear into pale surroundings.

Maintain accurate product colours.

No strong colour cast.

No teal-orange grading.

No aggressive cinematic LUT.

No crushed blacks.

No blown product highlights.

Clean modern digital image.

No heavy grain or vignette.

SHOT LIST — 8 SHOTS

0.0s – 1.0s
Product hook.

Introduce the hero product immediately through a natural action appropriate to the category: lifting it into frame, picking it up, holding it, putting it on, opening it, placing it into use position or another believable interaction.

The product must be clearly identifiable within the first second.

1.0s – 2.0s
Overall product form.

Show the complete silhouette and physical design using the most useful angle or motion for the product: side profile, controlled rotation, front-to-side movement, underside, rear view, opening, wrist rotation or similar.

Product geometry must remain identical to the reference.

2.0s – 3.0s
Macro detail.

Show an important real detail that actually exists on the uploaded product: stitching, texture, lace, clasp, zipper, hardware, logo, label, sole, dial, material grain, button, cap or another authentic feature.

Only show a logo, label or text in this shot if it genuinely exists in the reference.

Do not invent a feature just to create a more interesting macro shot.

3.0s – 4.5s
Primary usage.

Show the person naturally using, putting on, opening, fastening, applying or interacting with the product.

For footwear, this may be putting the shoe on or adjusting the laces.

For a watch, fastening the strap.

For a bag, opening or placing an item inside.

For perfume, removing the cap or spraying.

For cosmetics, opening or applying.

For clothing, putting it on or adjusting it.

For technology, performing one simple realistic interaction.

Adapt the action completely to the uploaded product.

4.5s – 5.8s
Finished use.

Show the product fully worn, positioned, applied, activated, carried or ready for use.

Make the product clearly visible and visually satisfying.

5.8s – 7.0s
Lifestyle check.

Show how the product fits naturally with the person's outfit and environment.

This may be a mirror check, outfit check, wrist view, bag styling moment, vanity interaction, desk use or another appropriate lifestyle shot.

The outfit must clearly make sense with the uploaded product.

7.0s – 8.5s
Natural movement.

Show the product in realistic motion: walking, turning, carrying, adjusting, opening, closing, reaching, applying, typing, pouring or another category-specific action.

Movement must be natural and physically realistic.

Do not deform the product.

8.5s – 10.0s
Hero finish.

End on the strongest commercial view of the exact uploaded product.

The product must be fully visible, correctly shaped, correctly coloured, correctly detailed, sharply focused and beautifully lit.

Any label, logo or text visible in the final frame must correspond exactly to what is present in the uploaded reference.

If there is no logo, label or text in the original product, none may appear in the final hero shot.

Use the most flattering product-specific angle.

A small natural movement may settle into the final position.

Hold the final composition.

Do not fade out.

Do not cut to black.

Do not blur the product.

EDITING

Eight shots.

Straight hard cuts only around:

1.0s
2.0s
3.0s

5.8s
7.0s
8.5s

No crossfades, dissolves, wipes, flash transitions, black frames, split screens, montage grids, speed ramps or unnecessary slow motion.

Continuous natural real-time movement.

AUDIO

No music, voiceover, dialogue or narration.

Use quiet realistic room tone and subtle product-specific foley.

Examples may include footsteps, lace movement, zipper sounds, clasp clicks, fabric movement, cap removal, spray, button presses, product handling or other natural sounds appropriate to the item.

Keep all sound subtle and realistic.

HARD NEGATIVES

No added logos.

No removed logos.

No changed logos.

No logo if the reference has no logo.

No added labels.

No removed labels.

No label if the reference has no label.

No extra label when the reference has only one.

No generic manufacturer labels.

No invented tags.

No fake stickers.

No added text.

No missing existing text.

No text if the reference contains no text.

No invented letters, numbers, slogans or specifications.

No guessed branding.

No replacement branding.

No generic product branding.

No branding added simply because the product category usually has branding.

No details added to blank areas.

No added or missing stitching.

No added or missing seams.

No extra or missing components.

No changed colour placement.

No changed material.

No changed texture.

No changed proportions.

No altered silhouette.

No simplified product design.

No beautified or redesigned product.

No inconsistent product between shots.

No mismatched pair.

No warped, melting or morphing product.

No duplicated product parts.

No random outfit unrelated to the product.

No automatic beige sweatshirt and grey joggers.

No automatic female model.

No automatic male model.

No wardrobe that hides or competes with the hero product.

No unrelated branding.

No unnecessary text, captions, subtitles, lower thirds, titles, end cards, watermarks or price graphics.

No malformed hands.

No extra fingers.

No distorted ankles or wrists.

No clipping through clothing or body.

No floating objects.

No excessive camera shake.

No fake lens flare.

No film burn.

FINAL RULE
The uploaded reference image is the single source of truth.

Do not use assumptions about what the product would normally contain.

If a detail exists in the reference, preserve it.

If a detail does not exist in the reference, do not create it.

If there is no label, there must be no label.

If there is one label, preserve only that one label.

If there is no logo, there must be no logo.

If there is one logo, preserve only that logo.

If there is no text, there must be no text.

If text exists, preserve only that text.

The finished film must look as though the exact physical product from the uploaded reference image was professionally filmed in a unique UGC campaign designed specifically for that product.

The talent, outfit, environment and actions may adapt to the product.
The product itself — including every logo, label, text element and physical detail — may never gain, lose or change anything under any circumstance.
